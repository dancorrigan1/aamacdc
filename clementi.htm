Peter Clementi
Vice Chairman

Peter Clementi has served on the AAMA-CDC Board for over eight years and is currently Vice-Chairman of the Board. A native of Miami, Florida, he has been in the Houston area since 1985 and has established his own development and financial services company. He has been an active home builder in "upper brackets" markets in West University and Montrose areas of Houston. His firm, Centriole Builders, also serves as Project Manager for several large Houston-based builders including SpawGlass and LDF Construction. During his Board tenure Mr. Clementi's firm oversaw the building of AAMA, Inc.,'s $4.0 million Multipurpose Education Center, which is the cornerstone of the George I. Sanchez Charter High School on the AAMA Campus. He's also been Project manager for the rehab of several AAMA buildings for code improvements for fire and safety requirements and he managed new construction of a residential facility that was added to the Campus. In these capacities peter Clementi drew upon his knowledge of the development and construction business including working with project architects, finalizing "as built" drawings, and cost overruns. This knowledge gives him a keen insight on how construction methods can impact on a facility's operating costs.

He makes his home in Houston and also has offices in Lafayette, Louisiana. Educated in Miami's Diocesan School System, he attended Dade County College. A successful businessman, Mr. Clementi developed his expertise through a "hands-on" approach to business entreprenurialship.   He is 39 years of age, is married and recently became a first-time parent of a daughter, Antonina Clementi.
