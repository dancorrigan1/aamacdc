Rogelio R. Santos
Interim CEO

Rogelio (Roy) Santos, Board Chair & Interim CEO of the AAMA-CDC, is an experienced senior manager, developer and negotiator, with over 30 years of practice in housing development, community revitalization and civil mediation and negotiation. Prior to establishing his management development firm he held senior management positions in career civil service with the U. S. Department of Housing & Urban Development (HUD). His background includes executive-level managerial experience at HUD Headquarters in Washington and in Field Offices in Texas. Since his departure from government, he started his own development and business consulting firm, The M&S Group, Inc. As a Principal Partner he is applying his professional knowledge in assisting clients and community organizations in the development of housing and community initiatives, and in resolving regulatory and compliance issues. The work of M&S focuses on multifamily housing development and community initiatives in inner city revitalization and economic development.

At HUD, Roy Santos worked in several capacities, in Headquarters in Washington, D.C. and in Region VI, including Deputy Director of the Houston HUD Office. His final HUD assignment was Deputy Director, HUD Region VI Office of Public Housing. He managed a sizeable portfolio of federal housing programs with over $1.8 billion in funding. During his leadership Mr. Santos led and managed a diverse professional staff of 200 + employees, including finance officers, real estate appraisers, housing specialists, engineers urban planners, and community relations specialists. A major part of this work included the funding of diverse real estate transactions, approval of housing agency budgets and resolution of community conflicts like demolition of Allen Parkway Village and West Dallas projects in Houston & Dallas.

Mr. Santos is utilizing this wide-ranging experience by broadening the AAMA-CDC's effectiveness as a community development organization. As Interim CEO he has expanded the CDC's work to include development of multifamily affordable housing through tax-credits, bond-financing; transitional housing and in economic development initiatives. This effort has increased the CDC's assets to over $20 million in 2003.

Roy Santos is an active community leader. He is a past President of the Board of Directors of the Association for the Advancement of Mexican-Americans (AAMA, Inc.), having served on the Board for 22 years, since 1981. He helped restructure AAMA's management team so as to improve its fiscal accountability, enhance fund raising efforts and attract a cadre of exceptional Hispanic professionals for volunteer work on the Board. In 1988 he became the first Chairman of the AAMA Community Development Corporation, a position he still holds. He founded the annual Bill Balleza-Fiesta Golf Classic benefiting AAMA's George I. Sanchez Charter High School. He is on the Boards of the Humble ISD Foundation and the Texas Association of Community Development Corporation (TACDC). He holds membership in the St. Mary's University Alumni Houston Chapter, the East End Chamber of Commerce, and attends St. Martha's Catholic Church. He is a Founding Member of The Deerwood Club in Kingwood. In prior years he was active in the League of United Latin American Citizens (LULAC) in Washington, D.C.

He graduated from St. Mary's University in San Antonio in 1966, with a BA degree in government and has extensive management training with HUD and at the Federal Executive Institute. Mr. Santos completed the statutory requirements for the court-annexed Mediators in Texas. His mediation work is in civil litigation and family law and was certified by the A.A. White Institute at the University of Houston - College of Business.

Married for 34 years, he and his wife, Mary Lou, are the proud parents of their 32-year-old daughter, Mrs. Valeria Marisol Durbin, and son-in-law Lt. Brent Durbin USCG. They recently became honored grandparents with the birth of their grandson, Thaddeus Alejandro Santos Durbin.
