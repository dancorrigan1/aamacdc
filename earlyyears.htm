Our early years.

The projects and programs that helped establish our development experience. These programs are closed and are presented as history.

Business Literacy Houston

A City of Houston-funded program, jointly administered with the Urban Business Initiative of Houston, that offered business development assistance to small inner-city businesses.

Educational facilities, 1999-2000

Development and funding support for Building C-2 for AAMA, Inc., including a proposal to Harris County Community Development and project and contract administration.

Multipurpose Education Center, 1997-2000

Support for the development of a 31,000-square-foot education facility, including securing bank financing, assembling the development team, and working with the project architect.

Building improvements, 1995-1997

Project management for fire code, health, and safety improvements to existing educational buildings.

Harrisburg-Wayside revitalization, 1993-1994

A two-year partnership with Houston’s East End Chamber of Commerce to provide training and technical assistance to area businesses, funded by the City of Houston.
