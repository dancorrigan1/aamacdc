Nicole Lemaster
Program Coordinator

She is the Program Assistant to the AAMA-CDC, and has served in this post since 1998. She oversees the day-to-day administrative work of the organization and is familiar with all of the CDC's projects and linkages with the business community. A native of Houston, Ms. Lemaster is a 1997 graduate of Pearland High School, and she is quite knowledgeable about community events and major happenings within the large Hispanic community served by our CDC.

A consummate worker, she is devoted to the CDC and is an invaluable part of our team. She resides in the neighborhood and is a proud Mother to her son, Joseph, age 7.
