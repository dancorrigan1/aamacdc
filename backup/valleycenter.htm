The AAMA-CDC Valley Center

Our Valley Center is located in Edinburg, Texas and was acquired by the AAMA-CDC in 2000. This is a former 21,000 sq. ft. commercial office building that's been retrofitted for use by a major social service agency under a long-term lease. This arrangement benefits smaller communities in the Rio Grande Valley through this agency's prevention & recovery programs for alcohol and drug abuse and youth outreach services. We provide a beautiful location with amenities that allow for temporary residence, large meeting rooms, adjacent office space, and ample parking. Our central facility enhances the delivery of much needed services in this part of Texas. The property is managed by Whitney Management and financing is through Laredo Nation Bank (Houston)."
