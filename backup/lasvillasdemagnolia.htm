Las Villas De Magnolia

The AAMA-CDC has just purchased
a 3.5 acre land parcel in Houston's East End in an extremely suitable location (intersection of Harrisburg & 72nd Streets). This is the future site of a 116 - unit Senior Citizen Center. It is an ideal neighborhood in which market demand and the presence of excellent amenities will lead to successful development.
