Welcoming Concord at Allendale.

December 17, 2003 · Community archive

AAMA-CDC celebrated the ribbon cutting for Concord at Allendale, a 264-home affordable housing development created in partnership with Trammel Crow Residential.

The initiative was designed to help families access attractive housing while lowering their housing costs.
